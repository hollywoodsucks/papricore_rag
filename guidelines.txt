During paprika harvesting, workers must use a small knife to cut the stem exactly at the natural node (joint) to ensure a clean and straight cut. It is strictly forbidden to leave knife marks, scratches, or any damage on the paprika body during this process.

Hourly harvesting performance is tracked and displayed on a TV screen in the break room cafeteria. The daily target norm (in kilograms) is dynamically adjusted based on the excess volume of paprikas available in the greenhouse.

On the performance dashboard, workers who exceed the target norm are highlighted in green at the top. Workers who exactly meet the norm are displayed in black.

Workers whose names appear in red on the dashboard are performing below the required norm, meaning their paprika cutting speed is too slow and requires immediate improvement.

In the modern greenhouses in Andijk, indoor temperature and humidity during the summer season are strictly monitored by automated systems.

At the packaging line, quality control is mandatory. Blemished, soft, or undersized paprikas must never be placed in export boxes.
